Before you begin

Istikhara is the prayer of seeking Allah's guidance in a decision. Pray two rak'ahs and the Istikhara dua first; this tool then applies the published Mustahsila-e-Qurani calculation to your name and your question (one matter, in simple words).

How the result is found

The letters of your name, your mother's name and your question are counted in Abjad. The total is reduced by the published steps to a Surah number, the number of Ayats in that Surah, and the relevant Ayat — all shown openly with a link to read the Surah. The worked example of the method (total 1960 → Surah 22, Al-Hajj → Ayat 10) is reproduced on the site so you can check the arithmetic yourself.

Read the sign with a calm heart

Take the result as a pointer for reflection, decide with counsel and effort, and trust Allah with the outcome.
