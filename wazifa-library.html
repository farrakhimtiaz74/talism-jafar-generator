For how many days should I read a wazifa?

The traditional course is 11, 21 or 40 days — regularity matters more than the count. Always recite Darood before and after.

How does the tasbeeh counter work?

Keep tapping each wazifa's gold button — at the target a green ✓ Complete appears and the phone vibrates.

Is this tool free?

Yes — completely free, no signup or fee.

كم يومًا أقرأ الوظيفة؟

المألوف تقليديًا 11 أو 21 أو 40 يومًا — والانتظام أهم من العدد. اقرأ الدرود قبل وبعد دائمًا.
